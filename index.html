ABOUT HAQ TRADERS & ENGINEERING

Practical Engineering. Reliable Solutions.

Haq Traders & Engineering is a Pakistan-based industrial engineering and trading company providing reliable boilers, burners, instrumentation, spare parts and industrial equipment to customers in Pakistan and abroad.

We focus on supplying reliable and cost-effective industrial solutions according to our customers' technical requirements.

We also specialize in sourcing and supplying industrial equipment from international manufacturers, with particular expertise in sourcing boiler spare parts, industrial burners and boiler-related equipment from China.

Whether you need a complete boiler solution, a specific spare part, burner, instrument or other industrial equipment, we work to source and provide the right solution according to your technical requirements.

With strong experience in the industrial boiler and engineering field, our aim is to provide dependable products, technical support and professional service to our customers.

Our goal is to connect customers with reliable manufacturers and suppliers while providing practical, cost-effective and dependable engineering solutions.
